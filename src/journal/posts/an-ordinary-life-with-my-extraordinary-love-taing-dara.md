---
title: An ordinary life with my extraordinary love, Taing Dara
date: '2026-10-09'
description: This letter was written as a reminder to myself and both of us to never take our ordinary days, our little moments, or the love we share for granted.
lede: To my dear husband, Dara. I have never wished for a perfect life, only a long one with you.
---

Maybe our life seems ordinary to others, but to me, there is something extraordinary about sharing this simple life with the person I love most. 

I find beauty in our ordinary day. Us watching reels, doing laundry side by side, riding our motorbike with nowhere to go, and sharing simple meals at home. I truly love our laughter and the quiet comfort of simply being together. 

Perhaps happiness was never about where we go or what we do, but in the little moments that become precious simply because I get to share them with you.

With you, I never have to be perfect. I don’t need makeup or dress nicely to feel beautiful. Even on the days I struggle to see beauty in myself, you make me feel loved, seen, and enough.

You are a man I deeply respect, proud of and love with all my heart. I hope life is gentle with us, we get to grow old together, and that even after many years, we will still find happiness in these little things.

If I could choose my life all over again, I would still choose this one, not because it is perfect, but because it is with you. I would choose every ordinary day and every imperfect moment, as long as I get to share them with you, my husband, Dara. 

Your only Love, 
Sourn Leakhena
