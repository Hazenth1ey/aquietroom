I love our ordinary days. The days when it’s just the two of us watching reels, doing laundry together, riding our motorbike with nowhere special to go, and eating at home. I love our silly laughs, our little moments, and all the simple things we share. 

It doesn’t really matter what we’re doing or where we are. As long as it’s with you, I feel complete.

Perhaps our life looks ordinary to others, but to me, there is something so beautiful about sharing these simple moments with the person I love most and it is a privilege.

I love that with you, I never have to be perfect. I don’t always need makeup, a nice dress, or to look my best. Even on the days when I don’t feel beautiful, you still make me feel seen, loved, and enough.

You are a man I deeply respect and a man I love with all my heart.
Your presence alone can turn an ordinary day into something beautiful. And if I could choose my life all over again, I would still choose this one—an ordinary life, as long as it is with my husband, Dara. 

Your only love,
Sourn Leakhena
